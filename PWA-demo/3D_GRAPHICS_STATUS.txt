🔧 TECHNICAL SPECS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  - uProjectionMatrix  (mat4)  - Perspective
  - uNormalMatrix      (mat3)  - Normal transform
  - uLightPos          (vec3)  - Light position
  - uLightColor        (vec3)  - Light color
  - uColor             (vec4)  - Object color
